Breathe

Power breaths, an empty-lung hold, a recovery breath. Repeat.
⚠️ Safety: Never in water, in the bath, or while driving. Sit or lie down. Stop if you feel unwell. Tingling and light-headedness are common; fainting is rare but possible.
InOutsec
How it works

1. Power breaths. Breathe in fully through nose or mouth, belly then chest, and let go without force. Follow the bubble: it grows on the inhale, shrinks on the exhale.

2. Retention. After the last exhale, hold with empty lungs until you feel the urge to breathe, then tap Breathe. Your hold time is saved. Longer isn't the goal.

3. Recovery breath. One big breath in and hold it (15 s by default), then let go. That's one round. 3–4 rounds is typical.

Best on an empty stomach, e.g. right after waking. If you have a heart condition, epilepsy, high blood pressure or are pregnant, check with a doctor first.

Cold Plunge

Get in, breathe slow, let the timer do the counting.
⚠️ Safety: Never plunge alone if you're new to it. Never combine with breath-holding (no Wim Hof breathing in the water). Get out if you start shivering hard, feel confused, slur words, or lose dexterity — signs of hypothermia. Warm up gradually afterward.
minsec
Tips

Exhale long and slow on entry to beat the gasp reflex. Start short (30–60 s) and build. Research (Søeberg et al., 2021) suggests around 11 minutes of cold per week, split across sessions, is a useful target. Warm up with movement afterward rather than a hot shower straight away.
